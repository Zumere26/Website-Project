Our Story

The Royal Whisk by Princess began as a hobby in Princess Damane's own kitchen. What started as baking for family and friends soon turned into requests from the wider community, and today it has grown into a home bakery she is proud to share with the town.

Every cake, cupcake and platter is still made the same way it started: by hand, from scratch, with quality ingredients and attention to detail. Princess believes a celebration deserves a dessert that looks and tastes as special as the occasion.

At a glance

  • Home bakery, based locally
  • Custom cakes, cupcakes & platters
  • Orders made fresh to date
  • Advance notice required for custom designs

How we bake

Quality starts with the ingredients: real butter, fresh eggs and good chocolate, not shortcuts. Cakes are baked to order rather than kept in stock, so every dessert is fresh for your event, not leftover from a previous batch.

  1. You get in touch with your order details and event date.
  2. We confirm a fixed quote based on the price guide.
  3. Your order is baked fresh in the days before your event.
  4. You collect, or arrange delivery, on the day you need it.

Lead-time guidelines

Because every order is made from scratch, please plan ahead:

  • Cupcakes and simple platters – at least 3 days' notice
  • Standard celebration cakes – at least 5 days' notice
  • Wedding cakes and elaborate custom designs – at least 3 weeks' notice

Rush orders may sometimes be possible – ask on the contact page and we'll let you know.
